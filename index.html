Ancestral Cross River Tradition · Calabar Hospitality in Rivers State

Where Ancient Forest Harvests Meet Culinary Craft

In Cross River culture, cooking is an expression of patience, care, and respect for guests. At Calabar Kitchen, our native cooks prepare every pot with shredded wild ukazi leaves, roasted uyayak pods, and aromatic local spices at 41 & 42 Ikwerre Road.

01.

The Sacred Art of Afang

Wild Forest Vine & River Harvest

Afang is a celebration of Cross River culinary tradition. Wild ukazi vine is hand-shredded into fine ribbons, pounded in a wooden mortar to release its earthy depth, and simmered alongside waterleaf, palm oil, and river periwinkles.

02.

Afia Efere: The Pounded Yam Blessing

Aromatic White Soup Without Palm Oil

Unlike red-oil soups, traditional White Soup uses no palm oil. It draws its velvet body from pounded white yam simmered gently with tender meat, roasted uyayak pods, and fragrant uziza seeds.

03.

The Spice Council of Calabar Pepper Soup

Aromatic Warming Broths

Simmered with native alligator pepper, roasted calabash nutmeg, uda pods, and scent leaves, our pepper soups are bold, slow-simmered traditional broths with deep herbal warmth.

Ancestral Ingredients

Forest Harvests & River Delicacies

Shredded ukazi vines, river periwinkles, roasted uyayak pods, and dried crayfish prepared in traditional earthenware.

Dining Room & Hospitality

Warm Wood, Terracotta & Unhurried Hospitality

Wild Ukazi (Afang)

Sliced into fine ribbons and pounded to bring out the signature texture and rich leafy depth of true Afang.

Uyayak Pods

Gently toasted over heat before simmering into White Soup, imparting an unmistakable warm, woody aroma.

River Periwinkles (Mfi)

Carefully washed and prepared native periwinkles adding rich coastal savoriness to every spoonful.

Pure Palm Wine

Chilled, naturally sweet palm sap — a classic table companion to spiced Calabar pepper soup.
